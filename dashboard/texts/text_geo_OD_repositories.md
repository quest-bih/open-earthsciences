This chart shows the distribution of open datasets across repository types and individual repositories where the research data underlying journal articles are published. Articles under 
consideration are (co-)authored by members from at least one of the three institutes within the Department of Earth Sciences at the 
Freie Universität Berlin and published between 2016 and 2024.

The tile size reflects the number of datasets per repository.

General-purpose repositories (or websites), such as Zenodo, Dryad, Mendeley Data, figshare or Open Science Framework (OSF), 
are publishing research data of various disciplines.

In contrast, disciplinary repositories (or websites) are usually designed for the needs of a disciplinary research community. 
For the Earth Sciences, the data repositories PANGAEA, GFZ Data Services or the data repository of the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI) are most used. 
61.2% of datasets in disciplinary repositories are shared under a DOI, unlike general-purpose repositories, which show nearly 100% DOI coverage.
