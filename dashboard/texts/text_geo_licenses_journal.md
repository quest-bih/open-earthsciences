Creative Commons offers various standard licenses that provide different degrees of openness to publications. Freie Universität Berlin strongly recommends that its researchers use the most open license - CC BY 4.0 (Attribution 4.0 International). This license allows researchers to openly share and adapt material for any purpose, provided they give proper attribution to the author, link to the license, and indicate whether changes were made.

This <a href='https://de.creativecommons.net/was-ist-cc/'>page</a> provides a description of the different licenses.
