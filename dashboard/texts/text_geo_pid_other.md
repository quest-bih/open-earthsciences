A Persistent Identifier (PID) is a long-lasting reference to a digital resource. PIDs can be created for journal articles, book chapters, research data, as well as for people, organisations, projects and many other digital entities. PIDs and their metadata are becoming increasingly important in the Open Science infrastructure. They are machine-readable and enable greater discoverability and transparency.
