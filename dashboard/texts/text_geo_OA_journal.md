This chart shows the Open Access share in scientific journal articles for the three institutes of the Department of Earth Sciences at the Freie Universität Berlin:

- Institute of Geological Sciences (WE 1)
- Institute of Geographical Sciences (WE 2)
- Institute of Meteorology (WE 3)

The share of journal articles available as Open Access is shown in bright colors (yellow, blue, and green) and is indicated by the dashed line. Closed articles are shown in faint colours (brown and grey). “Bronze” articles (brown colour) are categorized as closed access articles, as these articles lack a Creative Commons license and thus cannot be openly reused. 

This <a href='https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access'>page</a> provides a general description of the different Open Access statuses. The exact definitions used for this dashboard are described <a href='https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10998219'>here</a>.
