This chart shows the Open Access share in scientific journal articles for the three institutes of the Department of Earth Sciences at the Freie Universität Berlin:

- Institute of Geological Sciences (WE 1)
- Institute of Geographical Sciences (WE 2)
- Institute of Meteorology (WE 3)

The share of journal articles available as Open Access is shown in bright colors (yellow <span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background-color:#ffd966;border:2px solid rgb(0,0,0);margin-right:4px;"></span>, blue <span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background-color:#4373c4;border:2px solid rgb(0,0,0);margin-right:4px;"></span>, and green <span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background-color:#2e933b;border:2px solid rgb(0,0,0);margin-right:4px;"></span>) and is indicated by the dashed line. Closed articles are shown in faint colours (brown <span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background-color:#d7bb9d;border:2px solid rgb(0,0,0);margin-right:4px;"></span> and grey <span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background-color:#bdc5c8;border:2px solid rgb(0,0,0);margin-right:4px;"></span>). 
“Bronze” <span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background-color:#d7bb9d;border:2px solid rgb(0,0,0);margin-right:4px;"></span> articles are categorized as closed access articles, as these articles lack a Creative Commons license and thus cannot be openly reused. 

The Open Access category “<b>diamond</b>” is a relatively recent addition to common Open Access typologies, and its operational definition is still being refined. Consequently, diamond Open Access is currently displayed under "gold" <span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background-color:#ffd966;border:2px solid rgb(0,0,0);margin-right:4px;"></span>. For exact categorization see <a href='https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23813501'>our data</a>.

This <a href='https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access'>page</a> provides a general description of the different Open Access statuses. The exact definitions used for this dashboard are described <a href='https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10998219'>here</a>.
