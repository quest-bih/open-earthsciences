This chart shows journal articles for which the underlying research data are published openly accessible in a data repository ("Open Data").
Articles under consideration are (co-)authored by researchers affiliated with at least one of the three institutes within the Department of Earth Sciences at the Freie Universität Berlin and published between 2016 and 2024.

The bars are stacked by <b>Open Data status per article</b>:

- <span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background-color:#f0ba51;border:2px solid rgb(0,0,0);margin-right:4px;"></span> articles with Open Data mentions,
- <span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background-color:#303a3f;border:2px solid rgb(0,0,0);margin-right:4px;"></span> articles without Open Data mentions,
- <span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background-color:#b6b6b6;border:2px solid rgb(0,0,0);margin-right:4px;"></span> articles not screened.

In addition to articles that did or did not share data openly, the plot also shows 418 publications (19% of total publications) that were not included in 
automated screening because their full-text PDFs were not available at the time of screening. 46 articles do not have DOIs and cannot be automatically downloaded (2%).

The Institute of Geological Sciences (WE 1) shows a consistent increase in Open Data over the examined period.
